– Marketing View

## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is a Power BI business intelligence project designed to help businesses understand their performance through interactive dashboards.

The **Marketing View** focuses on analyzing market performance, sales contribution, customer segments, product performance, and profitability to support data-driven marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze marketing and market performance using key business KPIs.
* Compare performance across markets, customers, products, and time periods.
* Track sales, profitability, and market share.
* Identify growth opportunities and areas requiring improvement.
* Support strategic decisions through interactive visualizations.

## 📊 Key Metrics

* **Net Sales:** Measures overall sales performance.
* **Gross Margin:** Evaluates profitability after the cost of goods sold.
* **Gross Margin %:** Measures gross profit as a percentage of sales.
* **Net Profit:** Shows the overall profitability of the business.
* **Market Share:** Measures the company's position in the market.
* **Sales Quantity:** Tracks the quantity of products sold.
* **Forecast Accuracy:** Helps compare forecasted performance with actual results.

## 🔍 Business Analysis & Recommendations

The dashboard can help business owners identify the following opportunities:

### 1. Improve Marketing Performance

Identify high-performing markets and focus marketing investments on areas with strong sales and profitability.

### 2. Identify Growth Opportunities

Analyze customer segments and product performance to discover opportunities for increasing sales.

### 3. Improve Profitability

Monitor gross margins, net profit, and discounts to identify areas where costs can be controlled.

### 4. Optimize Market Strategy

Compare market performance and market share to identify regions that may benefit from targeted marketing campaigns.

### 5. Improve Forecasting

Use forecast accuracy metrics to improve demand planning, inventory management, and marketing coordination.

### 6. Make Data-Driven Decisions

Use interactive filters and visualizations to investigate business performance and make informed strategic decisions.

*Note: These are analytical recommendations, not verified findings about a specific company. Actual conclusions depend on the data and filters selected in the report.*
